6.6.22.5.3 fixes the Deadwood AROS SDK libc-header mismatch observed on the real m68k build.
The selected SDK exposes libc symbols to the linker but its stdio/stdlib headers did not provide FILE, SEEK_*, malloc or snprintf declarations to these translation units.
qrx_win_guest_loader.c now has an __AROS__ compatibility declaration layer, treats FILE as opaque, and avoids stderr/fprintf.
qrx_net.c explicitly declares snprintf for __AROS__.
No QRX86 execution/JIT semantics were changed.
