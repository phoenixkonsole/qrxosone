LIMITS / NEXT DEPTH
- Writer formatting buttons establish the compact ribbon UI; rich styled-span persistence is not yet a full word-processor document format.
- Spreadsheet formula engine intentionally implements a small safe subset, not Excel compatibility.
- Presenter has multi-slide authoring; transitions/media/master slides are later features.
- QRXAI is a local retrieval/rule assistant, not a transformer LLM.
