QRX OS One 6.6.22.1
Fixes the build-stopping assumption that every ReactOS ISO contains clock.exe.
calc.exe and notepad.exe remain mandatory. The third app is selected deterministically from:
clock.exe, mspaint.exe, wordpad.exe, regedit.exe, taskmgr.exe, charmap.exe, cmd.exe.
The exact selection is recorded in QRX:Diagnostics/REACTOS-THIRD-APP.txt.
The recursive PE audit now accepts the actual selected smoke-test set.
